Host settings

Tune display, input, and connection behavior.
On Windows with an Android client, DrawSplay follows the resolution selected in Windows Display Settings.
On Linux, change the resolution from the desktop's display settings instead of here: Settings > Displays on GNOME (search for "MetaVendor" if it isn't the tablet display already selected), or the "Virtual-1-1" display on X11 desktops such as Cinnamon (System Settings > Display).
1920×1080 at 120 fps is the default. Higher resolutions improve sharpness but may reduce framerate and responsiveness on slower links.
